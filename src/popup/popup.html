Bulk Delete for ChatGPT

Enabled on chatgpt.com

  1. 1 Open chatgpt.com and click Bulk delete chats at the top of the sidebar.
  2. 2 Click chats to tick them. Shift-click selects a range; Ctrl/⌘ A ticks everything shown.
  3. 3 Filter by title, or click Older to load more history.
  4. 4 Click Delete and confirm. It runs in the background while you keep chatting. Esc exits.
Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing leaves your machine except the delete requests sent to chatgpt.com.

Bulk Delete for ChatGPT

  1. Open chatgpt.com and click Select chats at the top of the sidebar.
  2. Click chats to tick them. Shift-click selects a range; Select all visible or Ctrl/⌘+A ticks everything shown.
  3. Use Filter to narrow by title and Load older chats to reach older history.
  4. Click Delete, review the list, confirm. Esc exits select mode.

Runs entirely in your browser. No data leaves your machine except the delete requests sent to chatgpt.com itself.
